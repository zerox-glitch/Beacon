## Integration notes (Android)

- All files are **transparent PNGs (RGBA)** — keep the alpha channel (PNG-32 or lossless
  WebP; never flatten or convert to JPEG). Copies in this folder are byte-identical to
  the uploads; no resizing or re-encoding was applied.
- Brand logos are trademarks of their respective owners, provided as UI assets for this
  app. Flower stickers and letter marks are decorative.
- Suggested resource mapping: `linkedin.png` → `ic_linkedin`,
  `flower-sunflowers-1.png` → `sticker_sunflowers_1`,
  `icon-blue-ring.png` → `mark_blue_ring` (rename after identifying).
- `twitter.png` = classic bird, `x.png` = current X mark — pick per brand era you want.
- `skype-classic.png` / `skype-modern.png` = two Skype eras (same, keep one or both).

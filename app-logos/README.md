## Integration notes (Android)

- All files are **transparent PNGs** — keep the alpha channel (PNG-32 or lossless WebP;
  never flatten onto a background or convert to JPEG).
- They are byte-identical copies of the source assets — no resizing or re-encoding was
  applied, so they can be downscaled to whatever density buckets the app needs.
- Brand logos are trademarks of their respective owners; they are provided as UI assets
  for this app. Flower stickers are decorative assets.
- Suggested resource naming: keep these file names, or map them to Android resource names
  (e.g. `linkedin.png` → `ic_linkedin`, `sunflowers-1.png` → `sticker_sunflowers_1`).
